About Ideal Weight

Ideal weight is a range that is considered healthy for your height. It's based on statistical data and provides a general guideline, but individual factors like muscle mass, body composition, and frame size should also be considered.

How to Use TDEE Calculator

  1. Select your gender

  3. View your results in the result section

Why We Use TDEE Calculator

  • Accuracy: Get precise calculations without manual computation errors
  • Efficiency: Save time by quickly performing complex calculations
  • Learning: Understand the formulas and concepts behind the calculations
  • Decision-making: Use reliable data to make informed choices

Limitations of Ideal Weight Calculations

  • Doesn't account for muscle mass vs. fat mass
  • Doesn't consider body frame size (small, medium, large)
  • Doesn't reflect body fat distribution
  • May not be appropriate for athletes with high muscle mass

Other Health Indicators

While ideal weight provides a general guideline, these metrics may give a more complete picture of health:

  • Body Mass Index (BMI): Weight-to-height ratio
  • Waist-to-Height Ratio: Abdominal fat indicator
  • Body Fat Percentage: Direct measurement of body composition
  • Waist Circumference: Indicator of abdominal obesity

Healthy Weight Maintenance Tips

  • Focus on nutrient-dense foods rather than calorie counting alone
  • Incorporate both cardio and strength training exercises
  • Prioritize sleep and stress management
  • Aim for gradual weight changes (0.5-1 kg per week)
