Why Water Matters

Proper hydration is essential for nearly every bodily function. Water helps regulate temperature, lubricate joints, transport nutrients, and remove waste. Even mild dehydration can affect your energy levels, mood, and cognitive function.

Every machine need fuel and body is like a machine that also need fuel. Its fuel is water.

How We Calculate

Our calculation is based on the standard recommendation of 30-35ml of water per kilogram of body weight, adjusted for activity level and climate conditions.

General Guidelines

  • Basic needs: 2-3 liters (8-12 cups) for most adults
  • During exercise: Add 500ml-1L for every hour of activity
  • Hot weather: Increase intake by 20-30%
  • Listen to your body: Thirst is a late sign of dehydration

Signs of Good Hydration

  • Pale yellow urine
  • Regular bathroom breaks (every 2-4 hours)
  • Moist mouth and skin
  • Consistent energy levels

Quick Hydration Tips

Start your day with a glass of water

Keep a water bottle with you throughout the day

Eat water-rich foods (cucumber, watermelon, oranges)
