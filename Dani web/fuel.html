Fuel Economy and Costs

Fuel efficiency measures how far a vehicle can travel on a specific amount of fuel.

How to Use This Tool

  2. Select any necessary options or units from the dropdown menus
  3. Click the calculate button

Why We Use This Tool

  • Accuracy: Get precise calculations without manual computation errors
  • Efficiency: Save time by quickly performing complex calculations
  • Learning: Understand the formulas and concepts behind the calculations
  • Decision-making: Use reliable data to make informed choices

Key Terms

  • MPG (Miles per Gallon): Distance traveled per unit of fuel (higher is better)
  • L/100km (Liters per 100 km): Fuel used to travel 100 km (lower is better)
  • Fuel Economy: Relationship between distance traveled and fuel consumed

Conversion Between Units

To convert between mpg and L/100km:

L/100km = 235.2145 / mpg

mpg = 235.2145 / L/100km
