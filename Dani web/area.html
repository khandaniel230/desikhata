Area Measurement

Area is the quantity that expresses the extent of a two-dimensional region, shape, or planar lamina.

Common Area Formulas

  • Square: Area = side X side
  • Rectangle: Area = length x width
  • Circle: Area = πr²
  • Triangle: Area = (base × height) / 2
  • Trapezoid: Area = (1/2) × (base₁ + base₂) × height t
