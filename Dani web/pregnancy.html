About Pregnancy Calculation

Pregnancy is typically calculated from the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP), which is about two weeks before conception actually occurs. A full-term pregnancy is approximately 40 weeks from the LMP.

How to Use Pregnancy Calculator

  1. Enter the last period date
  2. Enter your average cycle days length
  3. Click the calculate button
  4. View your results in the result section

Why We Use Pregnancy Calculator

  • To Establish the Estimated Due Date (EDD)
  • To Track Fetal Development and Growth
  • To Schedule Critical Prenatal Tests and Screenings
  • For Improved Prenatal Care and Planning

Pregnancy Trimesters

  • First trimester: Weeks 1-13 - Early development, organ formation
  • Second trimester: Weeks 14-27 - Growth, movement felt
  • Third trimester: Weeks 28-40+ - Rapid growth, preparation for birth

Key Pregnancy Milestones

Week Development Milestone
4 Neural tube begins forming
8 All essential organs beginning to form
12 Risk of miscarriage decreases significantly
16 Can possibly determine sex
20 Anatomy scan typically performed
24 Viable outside womb with medical intervention
28 Third trimester begins
37 Considered full-term

Only about 5% of babies are born on their exact due date. Most are born within two weeks before or after.
