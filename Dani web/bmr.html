About Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)

Your Basal Metabolic Rate is the number of calories your body needs to perform basic life-sustaining functions like breathing, circulation, and cell production while at complete rest.

Factors Affecting BMR

  • Body size and composition: Larger bodies and more muscle mass increase BMR
  • Age: BMR decreases with age as muscle mass declines
  • Gender: Men typically have higher BMR due to more muscle mass
  • Genetics: Some people inherit a faster or slower metabolism
  • Hormones: Thyroid hormones significantly influence metabolic rate

Why BMR Matters

Knowing your BMR helps you determine your daily calorie needs for weight maintenance, loss, or gain. To maintain weight, eat calories equal to your BMR multiplied by your activity level.
