About Factorials

The factorial of a non-negative integer n, denoted by n!, is the product of all positive integers less than or equal to n.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Enter a non-negative integer in the input field (maximum value is 170)
  2. Click the "Calculate Factorial" button to see the result
  3. For permutations and combinations, enter values for n (set size) and r (selection size)
  4. Click either the "Permutations (nPr)" or "Combinations (nCr)" button to calculate

Why We Use This Tool

  • Mathematical calculations: Quickly compute factorials without manual multiplication
  • Educational purposes: Understand the growth rate of factorial sequences
  • Probability calculations: Calculate permutations and combinations for statistical problems
  • Research and analysis: Support work in combinatorics, statistics, and algorithm analysis

Permutations and Combinations

nPr = n! / (n - r)!
