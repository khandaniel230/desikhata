How Exchange Rates Work

Exchange rates are determined by:

  • Supply and demand in foreign exchange markets
  • Interest rates set by central banks
  • Economic indicators (GDP, employment, inflation)
  • Political stability and economic performance

Conversion Formula

Converted Amount = Amount x Exchange Rate

Example: Converting 100 USD to EUR at rate 0.85

100 USD x 0.85 = 85 EUR

Historical Context

The modern system of floating exchange rates began in 1971 when the US abandoned the gold standard. Before that, most currencies were pegged to the US dollar, which was convertible to gold.
